Innovation Claim

What makes PermitGPT innovative and useful?

PermitGPT makes permitting faster, easier, and more understandable by converting complex municipal requirements into a guided AI checklist and readiness score.

Innovative MVP

PermitGPT is an AI-powered permit readiness assistant that predicts application completeness and approval risks before submission, reducing delays, resubmissions, and permitting confusion for homeowners and contractors.

How PermitGPT Differs

Existing permitting platforms often focus on application management after a user already knows what to submit. PermitGPT focuses on helping users before submission by identifying missing documents, explaining requirements, and giving users a clearer path toward approval.

Why This Matters

Faster and clearer permitting can reduce project delays, lower costs, improve housing development timelines, and make government processes easier for everyday users to understand.
