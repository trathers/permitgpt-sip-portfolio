SIP Project: PermitGPT

Problem

Construction permitting is slow, confusing, and inconsistent across cities and counties. This creates delays, added costs, and barriers for homeowners, contractors, and small developers.

Solution

PermitGPT converts complex municipal requirements into a guided AI checklist, readiness score, and approval-risk prediction before submission.

Target Users

Homeowners, small contractors, developers, property investors, architects, engineers, permit expeditors, and municipal permitting departments.

Innovation Claim

PermitGPT makes permitting faster, easier, and more understandable by converting complex municipal requirements into a guided AI checklist and readiness score.

Innovative MVP

PermitGPT is an AI-powered permit readiness assistant that predicts application completeness and approval risks before submission, reducing delays, resubmissions, and permitting confusion.

Prototype Features

AI-driven permit guidance
Permit readiness scoring
Smart document checklist generation
Approval-risk prediction
Jurisdiction-based recommendations
User-friendly dashboard interface
